JUSTICE ARINDAM SINHA
Judge, Allahabad High Court
======================================================

BASIC DETAILS
- Date of Birth: 22 September 1965
- Enrolled as Advocate: 11 March 1991
- Elevated as Additional Judge, Calcutta High Court: 30 October 2013
- Confirmed as Permanent Judge, Calcutta HC: 14 March 2016
- Transferred to Orissa High Court, took oath: 8 October 2021
- Acting Chief Justice, Orissa High Court: 20 January 2025 - 25 March 2025
- Transferred to Allahabad High Court, took oath: 7 April 2025
- Date of Retirement: 21 September 2027
- Source of appointment: Bar

CAREER TIMELINE
- 11 March 1991: Enrolled as an advocate.
- 1991-2013 (approx. 22 years): Practised principally before the Calcutta
  High Court, in Civil, Commercial, Arbitration and Constitutional matters,
  with a specialisation in Civil law.
- 30 October 2013: Elevated as Additional Judge of the Calcutta High Court.
- 14 March 2016: Appointed Permanent Judge of the Calcutta High Court.
- 8 October 2021: Transferred and took oath as Judge of the Orissa High Court.
- At the time of his acting-CJ appointment, he was ranked 42nd in the combined
  seniority list of all High Court judges nationwide, and 7th in seniority
  among judges from his home court (Calcutta).
- 20 January 2025: Appointed Acting Chief Justice of the Orissa High Court by
  President Droupadi Murmu, following the retirement of Chief Justice
  Chakradhari Sharan Singh (19 January 2025). Served in this role until his
  transfer.
- 6 & 20 March 2025: Supreme Court Collegium meetings recommended his transfer
  to the Allahabad High Court.
- 7 April 2025: Took oath as Judge of the Allahabad High Court following
  Central government notification of the transfer.

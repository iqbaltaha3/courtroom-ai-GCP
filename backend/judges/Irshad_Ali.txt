JUSTICE IRSHAD ALI
Judge, Allahabad High Court (Administrative Judge, Amethi district)
======================================================

BASIC DETAILS
- Date of Birth: 12 December 1964
- Enrolled as Advocate: 9 February 1989
- Elevated as Additional Judge: 22 September 2017
- Confirmed as Permanent Judge: 18 March 2020 (delayed - see controversy
  below)
- Date of Retirement: 11 December 2026
- Source of appointment: Bar
- Sitting at: Lucknow

EDUCATION
- Graduated in Law from Allahabad University in 1988 (1987 batch).

CAREER TIMELINE
- 9 February 1989: Enrolled as an advocate; practised on the Civil,
  Constitutional and Service side at the Allahabad High Court.
- 22 September 2017: Elevated as Additional Judge.
- 18 March 2020: Took oath as Permanent Judge - notably delayed relative to
  the Collegium's original recommendation (see below).
- Currently Administrative Judge for the Amethi district, sits at Lucknow.

CONTROVERSY - DELAYED CONFIRMATION AS PERMANENT JUDGE (2019)
- 5 September 2019: The Supreme Court Collegium passed a resolution
  recommending that Justice Irshad Ali (along with Justice Neeraj Tiwari),
  both then additional judges of the Allahabad High Court, be made
  permanent judges. The same resolution recommended a further six-month
  extension of additional-judge tenure for a third judge, Justice Rahul
  Chaturvedi.
- Shortly after, the Central Government issued a notification that instead
  extended Justice Ali's tenure as an additional judge by six months -
  effectively clubbing his case with Justice Chaturvedi's rather than
  implementing the Collegium's recommendation to confirm him. Justice Neeraj
  Tiwari, by contrast, was made permanent as recommended.
- Bar & Bench's contemporaneous report ("Has the Centre flouted the
  Collegium's recommendation to make him a permanent judge?") noted that
  under the Memorandum of Procedure, if the government disagrees with a
  Collegium recommendation, the correct process is to formally return the
  file to the Collegium for reconsideration, after which a repeated
  Collegium decision becomes binding on the government. In Justice Ali's
  case, no such formal return of the file was visible on the Supreme Court's
  website at the time, raising the question of whether the government had
  simply overridden the Collegium's recommendation without following the
  proper procedure - a notable episode in the broader, recurring
  Executive-vs-Collegium tension over judicial appointments in India.
- He was eventually confirmed as a permanent judge on 18 March 2020, roughly
  six months after the original Collegium recommendation.

NOTABLE JUDGMENTS
- Held that a registered adoption deed carries a statutory presumption
  under the Hindu Adoption(s) [and Maintenance] Act.
- Held that a government policy granting promotion to remove stagnation
  must be applied uniformly (April 2026).
- Held a Deputy Commissioner of Income Tax guilty of contempt of court
  (August 2024) - notable as a rare instance of a High Court holding a
  senior tax official personally in contempt.

MEDIA COVERAGE / CHARACTERISATION
- The most journalistically significant thread in his record is the 2019
  confirmation controversy, which is a useful case study in Executive-
  Collegium friction over judicial appointments generally, beyond being
  simply about this one judge.
- No personal ethics/corruption controversy was found in this pass.

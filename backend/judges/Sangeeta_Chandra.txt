NOTABLE JUDICIAL WORK (from LiveLaw case archive)
- Handled a challenge to the "UP Reorganisation Act," in which the court
  refused to implead the President of India and rejected a prayer directed
  against the first Chief Minister of Uttarakhand.
- Raised concern over the state government's failure to engage the
  Advocate General in a case despite court orders dating back to 2016,
  remarking it was a "very sorry state of affairs."
- Ordered an inquiry into illegal encroachments and makeshift temples in a
  Lucknow park.
- On the UP Lokayukta Act: issued notice on a former IPS officer's plea
  challenging an exemption provision for the Chief Minister, while rejecting
  a separate plea concerning the sitting Lokayukta.
- Directed the UP government to frame a scheme for the administration of
  Lucknow's ancient Buddheshwar Temple.
- Rejected a plea seeking to stop the release of the film "Jolly LLB 3" on
  grounds that it allegedly "defamed" the legal profession, finding "nothing
  objectionable" in it.
- Heard former IPS officer Amitabh Thakur's challenge to an immunity clause
  in the Election Commissioners Act, 2023.
- In a case involving a man questioning Rahul Gandhi's citizenship (a matter
  the court noted involved "pursuing cases against a very powerful
  individual"), ordered interim security for the petitioner.
- Directed the eviction of two advocates from the Lucknow District Court
  campus after their lawyer's chamber was allegedly turned into a "Vivah
  Kendra" (marriage/banquet venue).
- Imposed a Rs 25 lakh cost on loan guarantors for deliberately prolonging
  litigation over an auctioned property.
- Refused bail to alleged members of Sikhs For Justice (SFJ) accused of
  conducting a "recce" around the Ram Temple in Ayodhya ahead of its
  consecration ceremony.
- In a 1995 murder case, overturned an acquittal and sentenced three
  accused to life imprisonment, holding that "prosecution is not obliged to
  answer every hypothesis of the defence."

CONTROVERSY - BAR ASSOCIATIONS' CALLS FOR HER TRANSFER (September 2024)
- 28 September 2024: The Oudh Bar Association wrote to Allahabad HC Chief
  Justice Arun Bhansali seeking Justice Chandra's transfer, citing multiple
  complaints over her alleged "inhumane conduct" towards advocates in her
  courtroom, following an order in which she proposed initiating criminal
  contempt-of-court proceedings against Senior Advocate S.C. Mishra.
- 29 September 2024: The Bar Council of Uttar Pradesh passed a unanimous
  resolution supporting the Oudh Bar Association's demand, formally asking
  the Supreme Court of India to urgently transfer Justice Chandra to another
  High Court, and further resolving that she should not be assigned any
  judicial work until transferred. The resolution was copied to the Supreme
  Court for action.
- These developments occurred under CJ Bhansali's tenure and were reported
  as a significant and unusual public rebuke of a sitting High Court judge
  by organised Bar bodies. Available reporting in this pass did not confirm
  whether the Supreme Court Collegium acted on the transfer request or how
  the matter was ultimately resolved - this is an important open thread for
  further reporting.
- Separately, in a later development (31 May 2025), the Allahabad High Court
  rejected a plea seeking to oust a different HC judge's husband from a UP
  Standing Counsel post - reported under the "Justice Sangeeta Chandra" news
  tag, suggesting this matter was heard by or connected to her bench (the
  exact nature of her role in this specific matter needs direct
  verification from the underlying report).
- 3 May 2025: Allahabad High Court organised a two-day workshop on gender
  sensitisation for family court judges - also tagged to her name by Bar &
  Bench, suggesting a possible administrative/organisational role in this
  initiative (needs verification).

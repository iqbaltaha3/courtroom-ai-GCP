JUSTICE MAHESH CHANDRA TRIPATHI
Formerly senior-most puisne Judge, Allahabad High Court
Now: 50th Chief Justice, Bombay High Court (since 9 September 2026)
======================================================

STATUS NOTE: As of the May 2026 Allahabad HC seniority roster, Justice Tripathi
was the second-senior-most judge of that court. He was recommended by the
Supreme Court Collegium (6 August 2026) for elevation as Chief Justice of the
Bombay High Court, cleared by the Centre, and sworn in on 9 September 2026 -
he is therefore no longer sitting at Allahabad.

BASIC DETAILS
- Date of Birth: 21 June 1966
- Enrolled as Advocate: 11 January 1992
- Elevated as Additional Judge, Allahabad HC: 27 September 2013
- Confirmed as Permanent Judge: 10 April 2015
- Sworn in as Chief Justice, Bombay High Court: 9 September 2026
- Date of retirement: 20 June 2028
- Source of appointment: Bar

EDUCATION
- Graduated in law from Lucknow University in 1990.

CAREER TIMELINE
- 1990: Law graduate, Lucknow University.
- 11 January 1992: Enrolled as an advocate; built a practice mainly on the
  civil side.
- October 2007 onward: Additional Chief Standing Counsel for the State of Uttar
  Pradesh.
- Also appeared as counsel for: Kanpur Development Authority, Kanpur Nagar
  Nigam, UP Food and Essential Commodities Corporation Ltd (UPSFECC), Agra
  Development Authority, Moradabad Development Authority, and Kumaun
  University.
- 27 September 2013: Elevated as Additional Judge of the Allahabad High Court.
- 10 April 2015: Took oath as a Permanent Judge of the Allahabad High Court.
- Rose to become the second (and later, before his transfer, senior-most)
  puisne judge of the Allahabad High Court; presided over the High Court's
  principal bench at Allahabad.
- 6 August 2026: Supreme Court Collegium recommended his elevation as the 50th
  Chief Justice of the Bombay High Court (he would be only the second Chief
  Justice of Bombay HC to have come from Allahabad HC).
- Centre cleared the appointment shortly after.
- 9 September 2026: Sworn in as Chief Justice of the Bombay High Court by
  Maharashtra Governor Jishnu Dev Verma, in the presence of Chief Minister
  Devendra Fadnavis and Deputy CM Sunetra Pawar. He succeeded Shree
  Chandrashekhar / R.V. Ghuge (acting).

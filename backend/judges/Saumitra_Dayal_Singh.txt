JUSTICE SAUMITRA DAYAL SINGH
Judge, Allahabad High Court (Administrative Judge, Varanasi and Bhadohi)
======================================================

BASIC DETAILS
- Date of Birth: 20 December 1969
- Enrolled as Advocate: 25 November 1992
- Designated Senior Advocate: August 2013
- Elevated as Additional Judge: 20 February 2017
- Confirmed as Permanent Judge: 14 March 2018
- Date of Retirement: 19 December 2031
- Source of appointment: Bar
- Sitting at: Allahabad
- Administrative roles: Member, Administrative Committee; Administrative
  Judge for the districts of Varanasi and Bhadohi

EDUCATION
- Graduated in Law from Awadhesh Pratap Singh (APS) University, Rewa, Madhya
  Pradesh, in 1992.

CAREER TIMELINE
- 25 November 1992: Enrolled as an advocate; built a practice at the
  Allahabad High Court focused on Taxation, Industrial Laws, and Company
  Law.
- August 2013: Designated a Senior Advocate.
- 20 February 2017: Elevated as Additional Judge of the Allahabad High
  Court.
- 14 March 2018: Took oath as Permanent Judge.
- Has served continuously at the Allahabad seat since his elevation (per his
  own court profile, "since his initial joining in 2017" he has "dedicated
  his career to upholding justice").

NOTABLE JUDGMENTS
- 7 June 2025 (with Justice Sandeep Jain): Acquitted two men accused in a
  criminal matter, holding that "suspicion, however strong, may never
  replace proof," and that an extra-judicial confession is not of such
  evidentiary value as to deserve complete trust in holding an accused
  guilty - a notable articulation of evidentiary standards in Indian
  criminal jurisprudence.
- Active on the civil/procedural side as well - e.g., a January 2026 ruling
  on Order XXIII Rule 1 of the Civil Procedure Code concerning suit
  withdrawal (Jeera Devi and another v. Additional District Judge...).
- Given his practice background, his docket appears to skew toward
  taxation, company law, and civil/criminal appellate matters rather than
  high-profile political or communal cases - no controversy or high-profile

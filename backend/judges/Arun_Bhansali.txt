JUSTICE ARUN BHANSALI
Chief Justice, Allahabad High Court (51st CJ)
======================================================

BASIC DETAILS
- Date of Birth: 15 October 1967
- Enrolled as Advocate: 8 July 1989
- Elevated as Judge, Rajasthan High Court: 8 January 2013
- Appointed Chief Justice, Allahabad High Court: 5 February 2024 (sworn in)
- Date of Retirement: 14 October 2029
- Source of appointment: Bar

EDUCATION
- Studied law at the University of Rajasthan.
- Comes from a family of lawyers; described in interviews as a "third-generation
  lawyer."

CAREER TIMELINE
- 1989: Enrolled as an advocate; began practice at the Rajasthan High Court,
  Jodhpur bench, handling taxation, corporate law, civil law and constitutional
  matters.
- 1996-2013: Retainer for companies and trusts connected to the erstwhile royal
  family of Udaipur.
- 1999-2013: Retainer for Nuclear Power Corporation of India Ltd.
- 1999-2003: Standing Counsel, Department of Excise and Department of Medical &
  Health, Government of Rajasthan.
- 2002-2011: Panel lawyer for Indian Railways.
- 2004-2013: Standing Counsel, Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Ltd.
- 2012-2013: Standing Counsel, Income Tax Department.
- Also served as panel lawyer for Bank of Baroda, Canara Bank, Central Bank of
  India, Vijaya Bank, and standing counsel for HPCL-Mittal Energy Ltd.
- 8 January 2013: Elevated as Judge of the Rajasthan High Court.
- Over his ~11-year tenure at Rajasthan HC, authored more than 1,230 reported
  judgments.
- 27 December 2023: Supreme Court Collegium (CJI D.Y. Chandrachud, Justices
  Sanjiv Khanna and B.R. Gavai) recommended his elevation as Chief Justice of
  Allahabad High Court.
- 5 February 2024: Sworn in as Chief Justice of Allahabad High Court by UP
  Governor Anandiben Patel, succeeding Justice Pritinker Diwaker (the post had
  been vacant since Justice Diwaker's retirement in November 2023).

ADMINISTRATIVE / OTHER ROLES
- Member, Advisory-cum-Consultative Panel, National Legal Services Authority
  (NALSA).
- Member, National Court Management System Committee (NCMSC).
- Chancellor, Dr Rajendra Prasad National Law University (RPNLU), Prayagraj.
- Chairman, Administrative Committee, Allahabad High Court.

NOTABLE JUDGMENTS / JUDICIAL PHILOSOPHY
- Widely cited line from a compensation/dependency case: "A son continues to be
  a son both before and after marriage; a daughter continues to be a daughter
  irrespective of being married or unmarried" - used to affirm that marital
  status should not disqualify a dependent from compensation claims.
- Recounted in a public interview a case where he ruled in favour of a
  physically disabled woman whose candidature had been rejected by a
  recruitment board, even though neither the petitioner's nor respondent's
  side pushed for that outcome - cited by him as an example of the judiciary's
  role in protecting marginalised applicants.
- Has publicly stated that judges facing a conflict between personal belief and
  the law should recuse rather than let personal views influence a verdict.
- As CJ, has presided over an active Allahabad HC bench that during his tenure
  has ruled on a wide range of PILs (e.g., dismissing a PIL seeking to force UP
  panchayat roll revisions to follow the Election Commission's "SIR"
  guidelines; clarifying bank-branch relocation does not need RBI permission
  under 2017 guidelines; ordering forensic probes into an alleged case of
  advocate impersonation and forged signatures in a PIL).

MEDIA COVERAGE / CONTROVERSY NOTES
- No personal corruption or misconduct controversies were found in available
  sourcing.
- September 2024: The Oudh Bar Association sought the transfer of a different
  Allahabad HC judge over alleged mistreatment of advocates - this was reported
  under the "Chief Justice Arun Bhansali" news tag on Bar & Bench as a matter
  arising during his tenure as CJ (i.e., an administrative matter for him to
  handle), not an allegation against Bhansali himself.
- Coverage of his tenure is otherwise largely administrative/procedural (court
  functioning, PIL disposals, institutional events) rather than personal
  controversy.

PERSONAL
- Hobbies: philately (stamp collecting, a childhood interest since 1947-era
  stamps) and gardening; also collects autographs of people honoured on stamps
  or their family members.

JUSTICE SIDDHARTHA VARMA
Judge, Allahabad High Court (Administrative Judge, Prayagraj district)
======================================================

BASIC DETAILS
- Date of Birth: 19 September 1967
- Enrolled as Advocate: 5 February 1992
- Elevated as Additional Judge: 15 November 2016
- Confirmed as Permanent Judge: 23 March 2018
- Date of Retirement: 18 September 2029
- Source of appointment: Bar
- Sitting at: Allahabad/Prayagraj

EDUCATION
- Graduated in Law from Allahabad University in 1992.

CAREER TIMELINE
- 5 February 1992: Enrolled as an advocate; practised mainly on the civil
  side.
- 15 November 2016: Elevated as Additional Judge of the Allahabad High
  Court.
- 23 March 2018: Took oath as Permanent Judge.
- Currently serves as Administrative Judge for the Allahabad/Prayagraj
  district.

NOTABLE / HIGH-PROFILE CASES (from LiveLaw case-coverage tag archive)
Justice Varma's bench has handled several high-profile, high-media-interest
matters, including:
- The "Narsinghanand" matter concerning fact-checker Mohammed Zubair: the
  bench examined whether Zubair's tweets/"X" posts fell within free-speech
  protections while allowing a probe to determine if any offence was made
  out; separately refused to quash the FIR against Zubair (holding a "fair
  probe" was required) while protecting him from arrest pending the probe.
  This matter drew significant national media attention given its free-
  speech and communal-tension dimensions (linked to religious leader
  Narsinghanand and a controversial remark by poet/politician Kumar
  Vishwas).
- Stayed the arrest of cricketer Yash Dayal in an FIR alleging sexual
  exploitation.
- Granted protection from arrest to hairstylist Jawed Habib and his son in
  an alleged multi-crore fraud case.
- Quashed an FIR against Shaadi.com and its CEO Anupam Mittal in an alleged
  cheating case.
- Rejected quashing pleas from an alleged kingpin and co-accused in a case
  described as an "enormity" of a codeine-syrup ("cough syrup") racket,
  upholding the NDPS Act FIR.
- In a Chhattisgarh liquor-scam-linked matter, declared the arrest of two
  accused in a UP FIR "illegal," on the ground that grounds of arrest were
  not supplied in writing.
- Sought a DGP affidavit in a missing-person (Varanasi) case, remarking it
  was "disgusting that no progress" had been made.
- Ruled that once candidates are absorbed onto regular posts, irregularities
  in their initial appointment are deemed cured.
- Sought the UP government's reply on a PIL challenging a proposed
  demolition connected to the Banke Bihari Temple Corridor project.

PATTERN / CHARACTERISATION
- Taken together, his docket skews toward high-visibility criminal and
  quasi-criminal matters (arrest protection, FIR quashing, bail/anticipatory
  bail-adjacent rulings) involving well-known public figures (a Bollywood/
  sports personality, a celebrity hairstylist, a tech-company CEO, and a
  controversial religious/free-speech figure), making him one of the more
  frequently covered Allahabad HC judges in the general press, as distinct
  from specialist legal press.
- No personal ethics/corruption controversy involving Justice Varma himself
  was found in this pass - the controversies in the cases above concern the
  underlying litigants/accused, not the judge.

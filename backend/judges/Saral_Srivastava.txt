JUSTICE SARAL SRIVASTAVA
Judge, Allahabad High Court (Administrative Judge, Bareilly district)
======================================================

BASIC DETAILS
- Date of Birth: 29 October 1964
- Enrolled as Advocate: 26 February 1989
- Elevated as Additional Judge: 22 September 2017
- Confirmed as Permanent Judge: 6 September 2019
- Date of Retirement: 28 October 2026
- Source of appointment: Bar
- Sitting at: Allahabad

EDUCATION
- Graduated in Law from Allahabad University in 1987.

CAREER TIMELINE
- 26 February 1989: Enrolled as an advocate; practised on the Civil side at
  the Allahabad High Court.
- 22 September 2017: Elevated as Additional Judge.
- 6 September 2019: Took oath as Permanent Judge.
- Currently Administrative Judge for the Bareilly district.

NOTABLE JUDGMENTS
- Stayed a National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) order directing an
  Economic Offences Wing investigation (matter/context needs further
  retrieval for full details).
- Upheld the retrospective application of the Payment of Bonus (Amendment)
  Act, 2015 (April 2026) - a significant labour-law ruling with implications
  for employers' back-pay liabilities.
- Held, under the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, that
  the formation of a District Mineral Foundation is "beneficial legislation"
  that must be construed liberally - a notable pro-welfare interpretive
  ruling in mining-affected-area development law (with Justice Amitabh Kumar
  Rai, per LiveLaw coverage, Dec 2025).

JUSTICE SALIL KUMAR RAI
Judge, Allahabad High Court (Administrative Judge, Ghaziabad district)
======================================================

BASIC DETAILS
- Date of Birth: 8 August 1965
- Enrolled as Advocate: 10 September 1988
- Elevated as Additional Judge: 22 September 2017
- Confirmed as Permanent Judge: 6 September 2019
- Date of Retirement: 7 August 2027
- Source of appointment: Bar
- Sitting at: Allahabad

EDUCATION
- Graduated in Law from Gorakhpur University, Gorakhpur, in 1988.

CAREER TIMELINE
- 10 September 1988: Enrolled as an advocate; practised on the Civil,
  Revenue and Constitutional side at the Allahabad High Court.
- 22 September 2017: Elevated as Additional Judge.
- 6 September 2019: Took oath as Permanent Judge.
- Currently serves as Administrative Judge for the Ghaziabad district.

NOTABLE JUDGMENTS
- Delivered a widely reported split verdict in a case concerning an advocate
  convicted of murdering his wife in 1991/1991-linked proceedings: Justice
  Salil Kumar Rai voted to acquit the accused, while his bench-partner
  Justice Sandeep Jain voted to affirm the trial court's conviction and life
  sentence - a rare public disagreement on a serious criminal matter that
  drew coverage as "Allahabad High Court Divided On Advocate's Conviction
  For Killing Wife."
- Held that a school transfer certificate is not a certificate of date of
  birth, and that mere production of school records is not sufficient to
  conclusively determine a child's age (in a habeas corpus matter concerning
  release from a girls' remand home/shelter in Kanpur).
- Held that the Juvenile Justice Act overrides all other laws in cases
  involving a child in conflict with the law.
- In an interfaith-couple case: ruled that liberty "can't be curtailed under
  social pressure," ordered the couple set free, and ordered an inquiry into
  their allegedly "illegal" detention by police.
- In a case involving a 65-year-old Bareilly man allegedly held in illegal
  custody in a religious-conversion case, directed the Additional DGP, IGP
  and SSP to produce him, amid allegations of custodial torture since his
  detention on 20 August (year not specified in source snippet) - the UP
  government told the court he was not in illegal custody.
- Clarified that, except in "grave" cases, UP judicial officers need the
  District Judge's assent before filing FIRs in their personal capacity.
- Held that a contempt plea is not a means to "arm-twist or harass," imposing
  a Rs 50,000 cost on a party misusing the process (Feb 2025).
- Directed UP Police to deploy force to protect a 17th-century Agra "Hammam"
  (historic bathhouse) (Dec 2024).
- Held that mere appointment in Central Government services does not entitle
  a person to employment in State services.

JUSTICE RAJESH SINGH CHAUHAN
Judge, Allahabad High Court (Administrative Judge, Ghaziabad district)
======================================================

BASIC DETAILS
- Date of Birth: 18 July 1966
- Enrolled as Advocate: 8 January 1989
- Elevated as Additional Judge: 22 September 2017
- Confirmed as Permanent Judge: 6 September 2019
- Date of Retirement: 17 July 2028
- Source of appointment: Bar
- Sitting at: Lucknow

EDUCATION
- Graduated in Law from Lucknow University in 1988.

CAREER TIMELINE
- 8 January 1989: Enrolled as an advocate; practised on the Civil, Criminal,
  Service and Tax side at the Allahabad High Court.
- 22 September 2017: Elevated as Additional Judge.
- 6 September 2019: Took oath as Permanent Judge.
- Currently Administrative Judge for the Ghaziabad district, sits primarily
  at the Lucknow Bench.

NOTABLE JUDGMENTS (from LiveLaw/Verdictum coverage)
- Granted bail to a woman (Khushbu Devi) accused of killing her husband in
  conspiracy with her mother and alleged lover, citing that her husband had
  died, she was in jail, and her minor children were left in an "abandoned
  condition" - extending the benefit of Section 480 BNSS (the new criminal
  procedure code provision on bail).
- Held that a wife "outshines" an uncle in the "closest legal heir test"
  under Section 2(wa) of the CrPC (in a matter concerning who qualifies as
  next of kin/legal heir for procedural purposes).
- Held that a "No Objection Certificate" from a previous counsel is only a
  matter of "good practice," and that a duly authorised new advocate can
  press for bail without one.
- Held that a husband is his wife's "protector," and that his burden under
  Section 106 of the Evidence Act to explain her death sits on a "higher
  pedestal" - upholding a life sentence on this reasoning.
- Granted bail to a BJP worker accused of ferrying unauthorised persons
  from Bangladesh into India.
- In a case where minors were allegedly falsely implicated in a rioting
  case, directed the Bahraich Superintendent of Police to act against
  erring police officers.
- Granted bail to a person alleged to be an operative of Al-Qaeda and
  Jamaat-ul-Mujahideen Bangladesh, booked under the UAPA (Unlawful
  Activities Prevention Act) - a notably high-profile national-security-
  adjacent matter.
- Held that custody of a minor placed under a Child Welfare Committee's
  order cannot be challenged via a habeas corpus petition.
- Held that a declaration of marital status "strikes at the core of
  society" and can only be made by a competent court.
- Rejected a plea by singer Neha Rathore against an FIR filed over her
  social media posts concerning the Pahalgam terror attack, holding that
  the "PM [was] disrespected" and the investigation was "justified" - a
  free-speech-adjacent ruling likely to interest press-freedom/civil-
  liberties reporters.
- Granted interim relief to SRM University and its officials in an FIR
  alleging the institution ran law courses without proper approval.
- Took exception to the "seriously deprecated" practice of an advocate
  addressing the court without filing a vakalatnama (formal authorisation)
  or authority letter.
- Granted interim bail to a POCSO-accused person in an "interfaith relation"
  matter to allow him to look after a pregnant victim, with a scheduled
  review of his conduct.

MEDIA COVERAGE / CHARACTERISATION
- Justice Chauhan's docket is notably heavy on high-sensitivity criminal
  and national-security-adjacent matters (UAPA/terror-linked bail,
  immigration/cross-border matters, free-speech-vs-sedition-adjacent
  rulings around the Pahalgam attack), making several of his orders of
  particular interest for security and civil-liberties reporting.
- No personal ethics/corruption controversy involving the judge himself was
  found in this pass.
